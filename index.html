Countdown

A round-based action roguelite built in Godot 4 for a July 2026 game jam on the theme countdown. Playable in the browser — no download, no install.

Countdown

Survive five escalating rounds against two clocks at once — the round timer you have to outlast, and a vitality vessel that drains the whole time and is refilled only by the time orbs your kills drop. Between rounds you spend what you looted on weapons and relics that resonate with each other.
